ERROR / COVERAGE TRADE-OFF

What the system actually achieved

The guarded score combines a separated candidate with a participant-held-out risk classifier; display decisions use a threshold derived only from each outer training split. Rates must be interpreted together with availability.

A SCIENTIFICALLY INFORMATIVE FAILURE

Running remains unresolved

At running pace, only 2/73 windows are displayed and one of those still has a ≥15 bpm error. The prototype therefore cannot promise reliable exercise monitoring during vigorous movement.

This negative result is preserved rather than masked. It is a concrete target for future development, not a reason to imply that the existing gate has passed.

MISSION-RELEVANT EVIDENCE

Why self-monitoring requires context

NASA identifies changes to aerobic capacity, skeletal muscle and bone during altered gravity. Exercise is one countermeasure, and NASA's documentation treats medical monitoring and individual fitness baselines as separate factors.

That motivates Pulsar's distinction between measured optical rhythm, reliability status and contextual crew report.

2026 OFFICIAL CHALLENGE RESOURCE → DESIGN DECISION

Why uncertainty must change crew actions

NASA Human Research Program identifies altered gravity and distance from Earth among its five spaceflight hazards. Its cardiovascular-risk research explains why cardiovascular adaptation matters during prolonged missions. Pulsar maps these published concerns to an engineering response: make uncertain exercise sensor measurements explicit, withhold unsupported values, repeat or verify the reading, and document the result for established crew medical procedures.

TRACEABLE SCIENTIFIC METHOD

How Pulsar reaches a decision.

A direct chain from real sensors to a cautious display decision. ECG is used for retrospective scoring and training, never injected into a displayed interval's inputs.

01 / MEASURE

Wrist PPG + 3-axis ACC

Synchronous 20-second 256-Hz segments.

02 / SEPARATE

Motion prediction

Blocked out-of-fold regression on acceleration-derived nonlinear features.

03 / PROPOSE

Spectral candidates

Compare uncorrected and motion-separated peaks with subwindow stability.

04 / EVALUATE

Risk decision

Participant-excluded trained model, training-derived cutoff.

05 / RESPOND

Display / review

Record a self-check; do not translate unreliable values into health claims.

EVALUATION METHOD

Leakage-aware retrospective analysis

Outer folds hold out one entire participant. Each training partition uses a separate inner leave-one-participant-out process to establish the predicted-risk acceptance cutoff at a nominal 35% candidate coverage. The outer participant contributes neither training labels nor cutoff selection.

The resulting predictions cover 329 eligible sensor windows from eight development participants. The comparison retains all 332 windows in the overall coverage denominator.

NON-NEGOTIABLE LIMITS

What these results do not show

The cross-validated development study is not a preregistered untouched external test. Only eight participants were available, with correlated windows; uncertainty is substantial. The method can still accept serious errors or reject useful measurements.

The application is a research-data replay and self-assessment demonstration. It does not perform live astronaut telemetry capture, medical diagnosis, or clinical decision-making. Clinical or mission use would require entirely separate verification.

The original Gate 4 remains unmet. This hackathon release documents a narrower, achieved engineering outcome.
